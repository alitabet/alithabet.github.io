Selected Work

Selected generative AI systems, production deployments, and research-to-product initiatives.

My recent work focuses on frontier generative media systems, image and video foundation models, production AI infrastructure, post-training, and inference optimization.

Lipsync on Meta AI#

Led the end-to-end development and deployment of Meta AI’s production lipsync generation system enabling images to sing to music using state-of-the-art generative video models.

  • Directed an 8-person research team and coordinated execution across product engineering, infrastructure, PM, legal, and privacy organizations.
  • Built evaluation and quality frameworks for large-scale human and character lipsync generation.
  • Delivered one of the industry’s leading AI lipsync systems.
  • Served several million completed requests per day.
  • Drove a 7% increase in Meta AI sharing metrics.

MovieGen Product Integration#

Directed the post-training and productionization of MovieGen-based image-to-video and video-to-video systems deployed across Meta AI, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and Meta’s advertising platforms.

  • Led a 25-person cross-functional organization spanning research, product engineering, infrastructure, legal, privacy, and PM teams.
  • Operationalized frontier generative video models for production use cases.
  • Built production-ready post-training, evaluation, and deployment pipelines.
  • Enabled large-scale rollout of generative video capabilities across Meta product surfaces.
  • Contributed to significant increases in Meta Ads revenue.
