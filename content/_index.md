## Selected Work

<div class="work-card">

### Lipsync on Meta AI

Led the end-to-end development and deployment of Meta AI’s production lipsync generation system, enabling images to sing to music using state-of-the-art generative video models.

<span class="metric">Impact:</span> Served several million completed requests per day and drove a <span class="metric">7% increase</span> in Meta AI sharing metrics.

</div>

<div class="work-card">

### MovieGen Product Integration

Directed post-training and productionization of MovieGen-based image-to-video and video-to-video systems deployed across Meta AI, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and Meta’s advertising platforms.

<span class="metric">Scope:</span> Led a 25-person cross-functional organization spanning research, product engineering, infrastructure, legal, privacy, and PM teams.

</div>

<div class="work-card">

### MovieGen Efficiency

Led the efficiency workstream for the MovieGen family of frontier video generation foundation models, reducing generation latency from tens of minutes to seconds.

<span class="metric">Focus:</span> Model architecture, inference efficiency, evaluation, and production-readiness for large-scale video generation.

</div>

<div class="work-card">

### Imagine Flash

Led the technical strategy and execution of Imagine Flash, Meta’s real-time image generation system integrated into Meta AI and WhatsApp.

<span class="metric">Outcome:</span> Delivered the project from research to production in approximately four months, establishing Meta as an industry leader in real-time image generation.

</div>
